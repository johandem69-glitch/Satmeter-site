Why eggs are the most honest item on this site

Most items in a grocery basket move slowly. Eggs do not. In several markets they have doubled and halved within a couple of years, driven by disease outbreaks that have nothing to do with monetary policy.

This matters because it exposes something the tidier comparisons hide: when a sats price changes, you do not automatically know which side moved. If eggs go from 3,600 sats to 7,200 sats, that could mean Bitcoin halved, or it could mean an avian flu outbreak doubled the price of eggs. The sats figure alone cannot tell you.

Anyone presenting sats-denominated prices as a clean measure of monetary debasement is skipping this problem. Real-world supply shocks are constantly injecting noise, and eggs are the loudest example.

How to actually separate the two: record three numbers, not one — the local price, the Bitcoin price, and the date. With all three you can attribute a change to the right side. With only the sats figure you are guessing, and guesses have a habit of confirming whatever you already believed.

Protein per satoshi

Eggs are among the cheapest complete proteins available, which makes them a useful denominator for a different question: not "what does this cost?" but "what nutrition does a satoshi buy?"

A large egg holds roughly 6 grams of protein, so a dozen is about 72 grams. At a $3.60 dozen and a $100,000 Bitcoin price, that is 3,600 sats for 72 grams — roughly 50 sats per gram of protein. Run the same calculation on chicken breast, lentils, or a protein powder tub and you have a genuinely practical comparison that has nothing to do with speculation.

This is, quietly, the most useful thing on this page. Pricing nutrition in a fixed-supply unit is a real analytical tool, and almost nobody does it.

The honest caveat. A falling sats price is often presented as proof that Bitcoin "always" gains purchasing power. That framing quietly hides every period where the opposite happened — and Bitcoin has fallen more than 70% from its high on several occasions. During those stretches everything on this page got dramatically more expensive in satoshis. Nothing here is a forecast, and nothing here is financial advice.
