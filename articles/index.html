What ties these together

Every page here answers a version of the same question: what does ordinary life cost in a unit nobody can print more of?

The guides are deliberately unglamorous. Bread, fuel, a phone bill, a night at the cinema. That is the point — a Bitcoin price chart tells you about a market, while a grocery basket tells you about purchasing power, and only one of those is something you experience.

They also try to be honest about the limits. A satoshi price absorbs every real-world change, not only monetary ones, so it cannot on its own prove anything about currency debasement. Most pages here say so explicitly, because a measurement presented as an argument is how people end up making decisions they regret.
